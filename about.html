About SmartEco

Building interpretable, production-ready and intelligent ML systems

Our Mission

SmartEco focuses on building machine-learning tools that prioritize explainability, interpretability, and ease of use - ensuring that powerful models remain transparent and accessible to everyone.

Our Story

SmartKNN began as a response to a common frustration in real-world machine learning: KNN often produces intuitive and human-like reasoning, yet collapses the moment the data becomes noisy, imbalanced, or overloaded with irrelevant features. Instead of abandoning KNN in favor of complex black-box models, we explored whether its weaknesses were inherent — or simply a result of outdated assumptions. That exploration led to a discovery: the core idea of KNN was never the problem; the distance metric was. By rethinking how distance is computed and how feature relevance is learned, SmartKNN evolved into a version of KNN that is finally robust enough for production while remaining simple, interpretable, and transparent. SmartEco was formed around this philosophy: machine-learning tools should be easy to use, easy to trust, and powerful enough for real-world data without becoming a mystery.
